Free guide

Toronto home buyer checklist

Ten steps from pre-approval to closing day.

Get the free checklist

Enter your details to unlock the checklist instantly. Jade may follow up to see if you have questions.

Your 10-step checklist

  1. Get a mortgage pre-approval so you know your budget.
  2. Plan for the down payment and for closing costs, including legal fees and land transfer tax (Ontario, and an additional municipal tax in Toronto).
  3. List your must-haves and nice-to-haves, then pick your target areas.
  4. Set up listing alerts and book viewings.
  5. For condos, ask for the status certificate and review it carefully.
  6. Make an offer with Jade, including conditions that fit your situation.
  7. Arrange a home inspection and confirm financing within the condition period.
  8. Hire a real estate lawyer to review documents and handle closing.
  9. Arrange home insurance and utilities before closing.
  10. Do the final walk-through before the closing date.

General information, not legal or financial advice. Costs and rules change: confirm with your lender and lawyer.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to common questions.

Is the home evaluation really free?

Yes. It is free and without obligation. It is an informal opinion of value based on recent comparable sales, not a formal appraisal.

How much do I need to buy a home?

Beyond the down payment, plan for closing costs such as legal fees, land transfer tax (Ontario, plus a municipal tax in Toronto) and adjustments. A lender can confirm your pre-approval and the minimum down payment for your price.

Do I need mortgage default insurance?

A down payment under 20% generally requires mortgage default insurance. Rules change, so confirm current requirements with a lender.

How long does it take to sell a home?

It depends on the price, condition and the market. Jade will give you a realistic timeline with your pricing strategy.

Should I get a home inspection?

It is strongly recommended for most purchases, usually as a condition of the offer. For condos, review the status certificate too.

How do I get started?

Send a message or request a free estimate. Jade will reply to plan the next step, with no pressure.
